# Preserve neutralized Covered Passives until their trigger

When 變徵‧淨火 reduces a Covered Passive's duration to zero, the cards remain
face down and the effect is marked ineffective until its ordinary
NextPlayerActionStart trigger reveals and discards it with no effect. Public
Counter Effects without covered cards end immediately. This preserves the
existing hidden-information boundary and normal card movement instead of making
淨火 reveal or discard a Covered Passive early.
