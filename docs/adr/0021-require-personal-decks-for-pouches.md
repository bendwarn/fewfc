# Require Personal Decks for Pouches

The Pouch Rule Module depends on Personal Deck and cannot be enabled for a
shared-Deck game, despite the published Pouch rules not imposing that
restriction. This product boundary lets every Player choose privately and
independently from their own unshuffled Deck before the remaining Decks are
shuffled, avoiding shared-Deck selection contention and information leakage to
later selectors. Supporting published shared-Deck Pouch play would require a
separate future decision.
